Tonight's Music

Tim Falvey of Big Iron Echoes
@bigironechoes

Fusing Celtic-tinged vocals, poetic lyricism, and street performance grit, Tim Falvey delivers a tastefully emotive catalog of original Americana music for New York City audiences. As a soloist, the harmonica-wielding troubadour distills his semi-autobiographical catalog into a coffeehouse tapestry woven with tales of tryst and travel and torment -- with a few laughs along the way.

This Here Giraffe
@this.here.giraffe

Singer-songwriter from Los Angeles, CA. Lo-fi indie folk. Plans to release an EP within the next couple of weeks.

Sean Jeffers
@seanrjeffers

Once described by Steve Earle as “Almost like musical theater – except that I like it”, Sean Jeffers’ style is built upon character driven songs with singer-songwriter aesthetics. Originally from Maryland, Sean moved to New York City to attend New York University and pursue his master’s degree in music composition and songwriting.

Full bios, links, and tips are in tonight's program.
