‘mind-blown’ in new york celebrates all those big and small moments that our brains experience at an individual level. moments we can’t quite put down in words, but manage to blow our minds away, everyday…

Brandon Wisecarver
@brandonwisecarver

Brandon Wisecarver is a well-known fixture of the New York City artist's scene, coming to prominence for running the former Con Artist Collective - one of the largest art collectives in 21st century New York City. He is also a podcaster, writer, and a working artist in his own right, with a focus on tech and virtual reality.

Maggie V. Miles
@maggievmiles

Maggie Miles is a New York–based photographer and artist whose work explores intimacy, memory, and the quiet tension between observation and experience. Working across film and digital photography, she is drawn to fleeting moments—both the ones we deliberately preserve and the ones that remain with us unexpectedly.
