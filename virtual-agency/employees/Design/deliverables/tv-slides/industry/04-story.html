Our story

In honor of Russell Efros

Russell was a Bushwick musician, artist, and filmmaker with a gift for making everyone around him feel they belonged. It was his dream to have a space where people could create and build community. His family started the Russell J. Efros Foundation to carry his vision forward. We want to thank you for making this dream a reality.
