Dear Sprout community,
Today is Russell's birthday. He would have turned 34.

Like so many artists, Russell struggled to find his place, and we lost him at 25 to accidental fentanyl poisoning. We built Sprout Society so fewer people have to feel that alone.

Russell was a Brooklyn musician, filmmaker and artist who made everyone around him feel like they belonged. He was dreaming up a place for people to create, gather and support each other. That place is Sprout.

We aren't built to do life alone, and mental health doesn't happen in isolation. Social disconnection is linked to higher rates of depression, anxiety, substance use and early death. People who are socially disconnected are twice as likely to become depressed. People with strong social relationships have a 50% greater likelihood of survival. The U.S. Surgeon General has called loneliness a public health epidemic. It isn't a personal failing, and belonging has to be built.

That's what the Seed Fund does. It supports the community builders and initiatives bringing people together in intentional ways: support groups, arts and music programs, interest-based clubs, and gatherings where strangers become regulars. These builders are the solution, and funding them lets us reach more people, faster.

In January, I stepped in to lead Sprout. I've seen up close that for some people, Sprout is the first place in a long time where they feel they belong. People walk in alone and leave with plans to come back, or to host their own gathering. At our gatherings, intentional structure gives conversations room to grow into real connection. That's exactly how Russell brought people together, and he would be so proud.

Together, we've served more than 6,000 people, built 75+ programs, and invested over $150,000 through the Seed Fund, all while keeping our programming free or low cost.

For Russell's birthday, help us launch the next round of the Seed Fund. Your gift supports:
  • Seed Fund grants for community-led initiatives strengthening social health
  • Free membership at Sprout Bushwick, our Brooklyn home, and The Stoop, our online community
  • Affordable space for the community builders bringing people together
