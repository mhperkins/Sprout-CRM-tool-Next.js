Client Booking Calendar

v2. Scheduling starts on the hosting request form (/book). Someone who knows their dates picks them in a calendar popup; someone who doesn't skips it. After they submit, their portal link shows the same calendar with their confirmed and waiting dates, and that's where they come back to add dates later. Max approves every request. Names and dates are examples built from Floorwork's November.
