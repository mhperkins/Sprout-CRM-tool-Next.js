Accept does three things

Creates or updates the contact (Showcase type, Community bucket, how-heard filled in), adds them to the event you pick, and marks the application accepted so it leaves the New tab.

It feeds the program form

Accepted people are exactly who gets the program link for that night. Their bio and photo land there, not here, so this form stays short.

Its own sidebar item

Showcase sits between Events and Newsletter. Accepting is where you choose which night, and that adds them to that event.
