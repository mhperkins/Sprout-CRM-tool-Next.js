How the Email tab works. It reads hello@ and maxperkins@ the same way the 9am "Waiting on you" email does: last 30 days, automated mail filtered out, website form messages show the real sender. Each thread shows who, which inbox, subject, the latest line, how long it has waited, and whether they're in the CRM. Pin keeps it at the top. Completed clears it, and it comes back on its own if they write again. Ignore hides that one thread for good (not the whole sender). After you reply, a thread sits in Waiting on them; after 14 days with no answer it moves to Completed on its own. Instagram replies join this list once they're logged as Replied on Outreach (tagged Instagram); the app can't read DMs directly. Outreach also gets a Protocol tab: Run buttons that copy the phrase for Claude Code, and the playbook read straight from the repo guide. All three live in the CRM only; Gmail is never changed. The 9am email follows the same calls: completed and ignored threads drop off, pinned ones lead. The sidebar badge counts threads waiting on you plus new form submissions.
