What lands in your inbox

Program info

Their name, role and bio, their photo inline at 120px so you can judge whether it is usable without downloading it, and every link as a tappable chip. Handles become real URLs, and a custom label is kept, so mPATH's Spotify link would read "Spotify". Editing what they already sent sends a separate "Program update", which matters for the shared-phone case and for anyone fixing a bad photo.

Portal activity

A progress bar, how many required answers are in, and exactly which ones are still missing. When nothing is missing it says so in green rather than listing gaps. The host's contact details are in the email and Reply-To points at them, so you can answer without opening anything.

The flooding problem, and the fix

The portal autosaves as the host types

An alert per save would have put dozens of emails in your inbox while one person filled in one form. So pressing "everything I have is in" always emails you, and edits email at most once per portal every 12 hours, tracked by a new alerted_at column. Worst case is two emails a day per portal, however long they work. If that is still too much, raise the window or keep submit only.

Two lessons worth keeping

"Invalid OAuth state" is never a broken Google account

It means two workspace-mcp instances are running: one mints the sign-in state, the other catches Google's callback and rejects it. Nothing listening on ports 8000-8010 is the tell. Kill every instance, reload the window, and the cached token refreshes with no sign-in at all. Both your Google accounts are fine, and maxperkins@ can draft, send and read mail from here.

Pronouns do not get guessed from names

I wrote "her" into touchpoints and next actions for three applicants based on nothing but their first names, and repaired both rounds. The CRM has no pronouns field, so anything written there is a guess that persists and can leak into outreach copy later. They/them is the default now, in writing, in memory.
