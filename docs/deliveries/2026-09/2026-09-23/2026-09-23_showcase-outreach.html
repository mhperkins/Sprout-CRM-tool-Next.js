The Google auth lesson

"Invalid or expired OAuth state parameter" means too many instances, not a broken account

The cached token refused to refresh, a sign-in tab opened, and completing it failed. The cause was two workspace-mcp processes: one minted the OAuth state, a different one caught Google's callback. Nothing was listening on ports 8000-8010, which is the tell. Killing every instance and reloading the window left exactly one, and the cached token then refreshed with no sign-in at all. Both hello@ and maxperkins@ are live, and maxperkins@ can send, draft and read Gmail from here. The old note that this account's auth is broken was wrong about the cause.
