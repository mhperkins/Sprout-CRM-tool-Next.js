The Google Form is replaced.

Musicians and artists now apply at a Sprout-branded page that takes links to their work, audio and images, and lands every application in a review queue instead of your contacts. Nothing reaches the CRM until you accept it.

The link sprout-crm-tool-next-js.vercel.app/showcase

What people see

🔗

Links first

Spotify, SoundCloud, Bandcamp, YouTube, a portfolio — as many rows as they want. Most applicants send a streaming link, so this sits above the uploads.

🎵

Audio and images

Up to 3 files, 25MB each, for anyone without a public link. Uploads go straight to storage, so a big demo never hits the request limit.

📝

Four required answers

What they do, what they'd showcase, name, email. Phone, Instagram and access or gear notes are optional. No sign-in, no Google account.

🗓

No date question

The page says Sprout N Tell usually runs the fourth Friday and that we write back with date options. Picking the night is a conversation, not a dropdown.

What happens to an application

Step 1

It lands inert

Saved in its own table. Your contacts are untouched.

Step 2

You get an email

The pitch and their links are in the email itself, so most can be judged without opening the CRM. Reply goes straight to them.

Step 3

You review it

The 🎤 Showcase view plays their audio inline and shows image thumbnails. New / Accepted / Passed.

Step 4 · the only write

Accept

Creates or updates the contact, tags them Showcase, and adds them to the night you pick. Pass keeps junk out entirely.

The duplicate guard

🧬

A known email updates, never duplicates

Matching runs on the contact's email and their alt_emails, so a second address still finds the person. Their record is merged: blanks filled, types unioned, a touchpoint appended, status never downgraded.

🆕

A new person gets a clean record

ind_first_last, tagged Showcase plus Music or Art, Community bucket, warm, how_heard filled, pitch and links in notes, dated touchpoint, added to the event's people.

🚫

Junk never arrives

Pass and Delete act on the application only. The three test rows and empty submissions this CRM used to collect never reach the relationship data.

A bug found on the way

Second email addresses were being deleted on every save

alt_emails was missing from ContactSchema. Zod strips keys it does not know about, so the field survived in the database but was quietly dropped the moment that contact was saved anywhere in the app. Janaye, Camilla, Remy, Kingsley, Mac, Nina and Syd each carry a second address folded in during intake sweeps, and each would have lost it on the next edit. Now declared as a JSONB-only array field with a default, so old records without the key still validate and nothing needed migrating. The showcase queue relies on it to match people.
