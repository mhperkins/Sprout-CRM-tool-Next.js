Requests or not

Waiting dates get approved (the host is emailed). An event with no requests gets its own date and times written.

No duplicates

If the calendar already has something at that time, it's treated as this event: approved, no second entry, with a note to check.

Every date on the event page

A waiting request used to show only its first date. The event page now lists them all with their status.
