Glenn Dungan
About

Glenn Dungan is a 3D artist and writer based in Brooklyn, NYC. His work explores the architecture of dreams: the impossible spaces our minds construct while we sleep and the hidden logic that makes them feel real. Working in the open-source software Blender, Glenn aims to create surreal architectural forms that occupy the space between memory, geometry, and the uncanny. These landscapes reject Euclidean certainty in favor of emotional and psychological coherence, where distance collapses, scale shifts without warning, and familiar structures become strange. To turn his day from good to great, subscribe to his Substack!

Emily De Lima
About

My name is Emily De Lima. I am a Brazilian-American artist and designer based in Queens, NYC, working across various mediums. I graduated from Cornell University with a Bachelor's of Architecture degree and am currently a design and drafting teacher at the high school for math, science, and engineering in NYC. My work draws from mythology, folklore, science, and literature to explore questions of origin, identity, healing, and the ways people make meaning of the world. I am interested in how knowledge is passed down through stories, traditions, and craft, and how these histories continue to shape our understanding of ourselves and our place in the universe.
