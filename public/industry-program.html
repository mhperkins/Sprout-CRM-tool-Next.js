About

Maia Baird, aka the Emboss Babe, has been creating embossed cards, prints, and paper crafts for 20 years, a skill passed down by a beloved family member. Her vintage-inspired prints and meticulously handmade greeting cards are perfect for any holiday, occasion, home decor, or gift. Originally from the Philadelphia suburbs, Maia now lives in Brooklyn.
